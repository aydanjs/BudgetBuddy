# Turtle Budgeting Privacy Notice

**Effective date: November 15, 2026**

Turtle Budgeting helps people track a budget, plan ahead, and—only when they choose—connect financial accounts through Plaid. This notice explains what information the app handles and the choices available to users.

## Information the app stores on your iPhone

Turtle Budgeting stores the budget information a person enters directly on that person’s iPhone. This can include transactions, categories, budgets, savings and investment assumptions, goals, debt details, and net-worth history.

## Apple sign-in

When a person chooses Sign in with Apple, Turtle Budgeting receives an Apple account identifier and may receive the name or email Apple provides. We use this only to create and secure the person’s Turtle Budgeting bank-sync account.

## Connecting a bank with Plaid

Bank connection is optional. When a person chooses **Connect a Bank**, Plaid presents the bank sign-in experience. Turtle Budgeting does not receive or store the person’s bank username or password.

After a person gives permission in Plaid Link, Turtle Budgeting’s server stores an encrypted Plaid access token and the account and transaction information Plaid returns so the person can sync their linked accounts. We use that information only to provide account balances, transaction import, recurring-spending insights, and the other financial-planning features the person chooses.

## How information is shared

We share information only with service providers needed to operate the chosen features:

- **Plaid**, to connect financial accounts and retrieve authorized financial data.
- **Render**, to host BudgetBuddy’s bank-sync service and its database.
- **Apple**, to provide Sign in with Apple and device authentication.

BudgetBuddy does not sell personal information or use linked financial data for advertising.

## Retention and deletion

Budget information entered directly into the app remains on the iPhone until the person deletes it, resets the app’s local budget, or deletes the app.

For bank sync, BudgetBuddy retains encrypted connection information and imported data while the online account remains active. A person can disconnect an individual bank at any time. They can also choose **Delete Online Account & Bank Data** in the app’s Privacy & Data settings; this removes their BudgetBuddy server account, bank connections, encrypted Plaid access tokens, consent records, and imported server-side transaction records.

## Security

BudgetBuddy uses Apple Keychain for the device’s bank-sync session, encryption for Plaid access tokens stored by the server, and HTTPS/TLS for communication with the bank-sync service. No system is perfectly secure, so people should keep their devices protected and contact us promptly if they believe their account has been compromised.

## Changes and contact

We may update this notice as Turtle Budgeting changes. We will update the effective date when we do. Before a public release, Turtle Budgeting will publish a monitored privacy contact email and a hosted version of this notice. Until then, TestFlight users can contact the team through the TestFlight feedback channel.
